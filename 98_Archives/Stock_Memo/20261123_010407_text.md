/SAN DIEGO, Nov. 20, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Belite Bio, Inc (NASDAQ: BLTE) ("Belite Bio®" or the "Company"), a clinical-stage drug development company focused on advancing novel therapeutics targeting degenerative retinal diseases that have significant unmet medical needs, today announced that the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) has informed the Company that (i) it has extended the review period for the New Drug Application (NDA) for tinlarebant for the treatment of Stargardt disease type 1 (STGD1) by three months, with the extended Prescription Drug User Fee Act (PDUFA) action date being May 12, 2027, and (ii) if no major deficiencies are identified during FDA's ongoing review, the FDA plans to communicate proposed labeling requests and any anticipated postmarketing requirement requests, if necessary, with the Company by April 12, 2027.

聖地亞哥，2026年11月20日（環球新聞社）——貝利特生物公司（納斯達克代碼：BLTE）（「Belite Bio®」或「公司」）是一家處於臨床階段的藥物研發企業，專注於開發針對退行性視網膜疾病的新型療法，以滿足該領域尚未被充分滿足的重大醫療需求。公司今日宣佈，美國食品藥品監督管理局（FDA）已通知公司：(i) 將tinlarebant用於治療1型斯塔加特病（STGD1）的新藥上市申請（NDA）的審評期限延長三個月，修訂後的《處方藥使用者付費法案》（PDUFA）行動日期爲2027年5月12日；以及(ii) 若FDA在當前審評過程中未發現重大缺陷，FDA計劃於2027年4月12日前與公司溝通擬議的藥品說明書相關要求及必要的上市後監管要求。

The extension provides the FDA with additional time to review information provided in support of the NDA. Belite Bio remains committed to working closely with FDA throughout the review process, and is confident in the clinical evidence supporting tinlarebant and its potential to address the significant unmet medical need in the STGD1 patient population.

該延期爲美國食品藥品監督管理局（FDA）提供了更多時間，以審評支持新藥上市申請（NDA）的相關資料。Belite Bio始終致力於在整個審評過程中與FDA保持密切合作，並對支持tinlarebant的臨床證據及其在解決STGD1患者群體中尚未滿足的重大醫療需求方面的潛力充滿信心。

About Tinlarebant (a/k/a LBS-008)

關於Tinlarebant（又名LBS-008）

Tinlarebant is a novel oral therapy that is intended to reduce the accumulation of vitamin A-based toxins (known as bisretinoids) that cause retinal disease in Stargardt disease type 1 (STGD1) and also contribute to disease progression in geographic atrophy (GA), or advanced dry age-related macular degeneration (AMD). Bisretinoids are by-products of the visual cycle, which is dependent on the supply of vitamin A (retinol) to the eye. Tinlarebant works by reducing and maintaining levels of serum retinol binding protein 4 (RBP4), the sole carrier protein for retinol transport from the liver to the eye. By modulating the amount of retinol entering the eye, tinlarebant reduces the formation of bisretinoids. Tinlarebant has been granted Breakthrough Therapy Designation, Fast Track Designation, and Rare Pediatric Disease Designation in the U.S., Orphan Drug Designation in the U.S., Europe, Japan, and Switzerland, and Sakigake Designation in Japan for the treatment of STGD1.

Tinlarebant是一種新型口服療法，旨在減少維生素A衍生物類毒素（即雙視黃醇類化合物）的蓄積。這類毒素是導致Stargardt病1型（STGD1）視網膜病變的病因，並且還參與地理萎縮（GA），即晚期乾性年齡相關性黃斑變性（AMD）的疾病進展。雙視黃醇類化合物是視覺循環的代謝產物，而該循環依賴於視網膜從眼內獲得維生素A（視黃醇）。Tinlarebant通過降低並維持血清視黃醇結合蛋白4（RBP4）的水平發揮作用，RBP4是視黃醇從肝臟轉運至眼內的唯一載體蛋白。通過調節進入眼內的視黃醇量，Tinlarebant可減少雙視黃醇類化合物的生成。Tinlarebant已在美國獲準突破性療法認定、快速通道認定及罕見兒科疾病認定，在美國、歐洲、日本和瑞士獲得孤兒藥認定，並在日本獲準「先驅藥物」認定，用於治療STGD1。

About Belite Bio

關於貝萊特生物

Belite Bio is a clinical-stage drug development company focused on advancing novel therapeutics targeting degenerative retinal diseases that have significant unmet medical needs, such as Stargardt disease type 1 (STGD1) and geographic atrophy (GA) in advanced dry age-related macular degeneration (AMD), in addition to specific metabolic diseases. Belite Bio's lead candidate, tinlarebant, is an oral therapy intended to reduce the accumulation of bisretinoid toxins in the eye.
